Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam

“The Missile Man of India”

A great scientist, teacher, author, and the 11th President of India.

About Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam

Dr. Avul Pakir Jainulabdeen Abdul Kalam was born on 15 October 1931 in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu. He came from a humble family and developed a strong interest in science and learning from an early age.

Kalam studied aerospace engineering and later joined India's space and defence research programmes. He played an important role in the development of India's space and missile technologies and became widely respected for his contribution to the country's scientific progress.

In 2002, he became the 11th President of India. During his presidency, he became especially popular among students and young people because of his simple personality, inspiring speeches, and strong belief in education.

Dr. Kalam was also a prolific writer. His books encouraged young Indians to dream big, work hard, and contribute to the development of the nation. He continued teaching and interacting with students even after leaving the presidency. He passed away on 27 July 2015 while delivering a lecture at IIM Shillong.
